## Global visualization for the susceptibility to weakness from the combined effects of reduced *I<sub>Kir</sub>* and varying [K<sup>+</sup>]<sub>o</sub>, over a wide range of both variables

(A) Representative IV plots for low, normal, and high K<sup>+</sup> show the shift of *V<sub>rest</sub>* (circles) and the possibility for two values of *V<sub>rest</sub>* (dark blue circles, top panel) with an intervening unstable equilibrium point (light blue circle). These *V<sub>rest</sub>* values (along with many other values for a full range of [K+]O) are then plotted as a function of [K+]O to generate the phase plot (smooth blue curve, right panel). This diagram summarizes all the possible *V<sub>rest</sub>* outcomes, for a single, a priori selected scaling factor for *I<sub>Kir</sub>*. 

(B) The process of generating a V_rest – [K+]O phase plot is then repeated for a range of scale factors for *I<sub>Kir</sub>*, with each curve being superimposed on a global phase diagram (top panel, scale varying from 1.0 for WT in blue, to severe loss in red). Select cases are illustrated for moderate loss of I_Kir (scale = 0.20, middle) and a severe loss (scale = 0.08, bottom). For these cases with reduced *I<sub>Kir</sub>*, the possible value of V_rest “falls off the island” near the center of the plot and jumps to a depolarized value of about -50 mV, as [K+]O is increased > 6 mM, or for the case with a severe reduction (scale = 0.08, bottom) when [K+]O < 4.0 mM. 

(C) A summary phase portrait of [K+]O vs scale I_Kir can be constructed from the analysis in B to show the borders between regions where V_rest is normally polarized with preservation of contractility (white region) or depolarized with weakness (red and blue regions for high-K and low-K, respectively). For scale *I<sub>Kir</sub>*  ≥ 0.35, which includes the haploinsufficiency case of 0.5, contractility is preserved over the entire range of [K+]O from 2.0 mM to 7.5 mM. For scale = 0.28, as in Kir2.1cKO/cKO soleus muscle, the model predicts susceptibility to high-K, but not low-K. A severe reduction of *I<sub>Kir</sub>* (e.g. scale = 0.08) predicts susceptibility to both high-K and low-K. For extreme loss of *I<sub>Kir</sub>* with scale < 0.06, the model fails to have a normally polarized V_rest for any value of [K+]O which would be lethal with no contraction (gray region).
