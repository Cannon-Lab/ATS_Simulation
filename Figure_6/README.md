## K-Dependence of Fiber Excitability (Time Domain)

 ## The simulated reduction of _I<sub>Kir</sub>_  is progressively more severe for each row, starting from a relative scaling of 1.00 for WT (top row) down to 0.07 in the simuation of block by 50 &mu;M Ba<sup>2+</sup> (bottom row).
  
(A) Each panel shows the voltage response of a simulated fiber that was stimulated once every 120 sec by activation of the motor endplate (0.5 msec conductance increase). On this compressed time scale, an action potential appears as a vertical line. Simulations were performed for a step decrease in [K<sup>+</sup>]<sub>o</sub> (left column 4.5 &#8594; 2.0 mM) or for a step increase in [K<sup>+</sup>]<sub>o</sub> (right column 4.5 &#8594; 8.0 mM). The yellow shading indicates times at which the peak voltage response fails to exceed -20 mV (dashed line) and therefore is predicted to severely impair contraction because of insufficient Ca<sup>2+</sup> release. 

(B) Voltage responses are shown on an expanded time scale to illustrate the waveform of the triggered action potential (e.g. top row) or subthreshold endplate potential (e.g. bottom row when [K<sup>+</sup>]<sub>o</sub> is 2.0 or 8.0 mM). The fine dashed line in each row show shows the initial resting potential in 4.5 mM [K<sup>+</sup>]<sub>o</sub>; the heavy dashed line depicts the -20 mV threshold for contraction.
